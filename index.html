Our Non-Profit Mission

Building a stronger, accessible internet for everyone through community-driven initiatives.

Community Driven

MoeDove LLC stands apart from traditional commercial entities. We are a community-driven organization dedicated to strengthening the global digital infrastructure. Our primary goal is not profit, but the advancement of network stability, accessibility, and education.

Infrastructure & Education

We proudly host the RIPE NCC K-ROOT node in Hong Kong to bolster regional DNS resilience. Furthermore, we provide free BGP Virtual Machines to researchers and enthusiasts, fostering the next generation of network engineers.

Affordable Resources

To support our community, we also offer RIPE and ARIN LIR services (ASN registration and IP allocation) strictly at cost price. We believe in open, affordable access to internet resources for everyone.
